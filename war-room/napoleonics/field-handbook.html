Field Handbook(Napoleonic Wars)

Your campaign cheat sheet: the essential rules and roles for fast reference in play. For full detail, see the Officer’s Manual.

Overall Summary

  • Game begins March 25, 1802, immediately after the signing of the Treaty of Amiens.
  • Nine nations available for player control: Great Britain, France, Prussia, Austria, Spain, the Ottoman Empire, USA, Sweden, and Russia.
  • Doctrine of Historical Plausibility — all actions must be era-appropriate. Admins may halt or overturn ahistorical moves. Research your nation’s history.
  • Nothing is official unless posted in the correct threads. Orders that have been edited will not be accepted.
  • One turn is approximately one month in-game. Submit all orders each turn.
  • All existing historical treaties as of March 25, 1802 are in full effect at game start.

All Roles

  • Act within your role’s responsibilities and follow the chain of command.
  • All official diplomatic negotiations must be conducted on the forum, either publicly or by PM with the Admin included. Off-site agreements are invalid.
  • Troops take 3 months to raise and be ready for active duty.
  • Upgrade weapons and equipment whenever possible for stronger units.
  • Guard regiments are earned through battlefield experience — they cannot be directly recruited.

Leader

  • Set national policies and determine all official strategies.
  • Command armies personally in major battles and campaigns.
  • Appoint, promote, or dismiss generals, admirals, and diplomats.
  • Post all official government, military, budget, and diplomacy orders — with final say.
  • Coordinate with Admin for all legislation, treaties, and law changes.

General or Admiral

  • Lead armies or fleets as assigned. Post movement and battle orders in proper threads.
  • Follow the leader’s strategic goals or pursue your own — at risk of dismissal.
  • Reinforce, merge, split, and organize field units as needed. Reinforcing regiments must match type and equipment of the reinforced regiment.
  • Promotions and dismissals are at the Leader’s discretion.

Finances & Budget

  • Admins post an annual Financial Statement listing revenues, debts, and interest.
  • Gold represents your military budget after mandatory expenses. It can be saved year-to-year, but never beyond the current year’s total revenue.
  • Full upkeep on all active units and debt interest must be paid as of January 1 each year.
  • You may borrow money, raise taxes (with parliament consent if applicable), or exploit conquered territories to increase revenue.
  • You may transfer Gold to other powers — post the amount, recipient, and notify Admin immediately.

Casus Belli & Treaties

  • You must have a valid casus belli to declare war. Wars launched without formal declaration and/or without proving diplomatic or political cause may result in domestic unrest or other unforeseen consequences.
  • Players may manufacture claims and casus belli via game mechanics — you need not wait for a treaty to be broken.
  • All treaties must be negotiated and posted with Admin included. Nothing takes effect until the treaty is ratified by the Admin.
  • Secret clauses are permitted in treaties but must be included in the Admin PM at signing. Secret clauses revealed after the fact are not binding.

Raising Troops & Combat Stats

  • Troop types: Line Infantry, Light Infantry, Irregulars, Heavy Cavalry, Light Cavalry, Artillery Batteries, Horse Artillery, Siege Batteries, and nation-specific options.
  • All new regiments begin with inferior weapons (-2 stat) unless upgraded at additional cost.
  • Experience:
    • 1-2: Green, some training
    • 3-4: Trained, may have been under fire
    • 5-6: Been through a campaign
    • 7-8: Grizzled veterans
    • 9-10: Lucky survivors
  • Morale:
    • 1-2: Desperate, plotting mutiny or desertion
    • 3-4: Disillusioned, prone to desertion
    • 5-6: Shaken, shell-shocked, or bored
    • 7-8: High-spirited, eager
    • 9-10: Patriotic exuberance!
  • Weapons:
    • -2: Unarmed or pike
    • -1: Smoothbore matchlock
    • 0: Smoothbore musket
    • +1: Rifled musket
    • +2: Needler rifle

National Bonuses

  • France — Units gain experience faster (the "Immortals" guard system).
  • Britain — May recruit Congreve rocket artillery.
  • Prussia — May recruit superior line infantry.
  • Austria — Council of Electors votes increase legitimacy and reduce rebellion risk.
  • Russia — May recruit Cossack light cavalry.
  • Spain — Native and foreign auxiliaries are more effective in battle.
  • Ottomans — May recruit the Dardanelles Gun (2,000 lb siege cannon).
  • Sweden — May recruit marine light infantry, especially effective in naval and amphibious operations.
