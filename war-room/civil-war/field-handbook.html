Field Handbook(Civil War)

Your campaign cheat sheet: the essential rules and roles for fast reference in play. For full detail, see the Officer’s Manual.

Overall Summary

  • Game begins April 13, 1861 (post-Fort Sumter). Decisions must be somewhat plausible to the era — admins may decline completely ahistorical moves, but latitude will be granted.
  • Play as Union or Confederacy teams, each with a Leader and supporting roles.
  • Official orders (military, budget, diplomacy) only count if posted in proper threads, and no edits are permitted once posted.
  • One turn is approximately one month in-game. Submit all orders for each turn.

Victory Conditions

  • Union:
    • Do not lose Washington, D.C.
    • Capture Richmond & Vicksburg before Jan 1, 1866.
    • Hold Cincinnati, OH on Jan 1, 1866.
  • Confederacy:
    • Survive until Jan 1, 1866.
    • Hold Richmond and/or Vicksburg on Jan 1, 1866.
    • Never lose both Richmond & Vicksburg at the same time.
  • Secondary & secret objectives exist — complete these to earn rewards.

All Roles

  • Act within your role's responsibilities, and follow the chain of command.
  • It takes 3 months to train and equip new regiments/batteries.
  • It is recommended to upgrade weapons/equipment when possible for stronger units.
  • Guard regiments are earned through battlefield experience, not direct recruitment.
  • Don't neglect diplomacy! Mercenaries and other features may only be possible via treaty.
  • Admins control plausibility and may halt actions; check Discord/full rules for cost and event updates.

Leader

  • Set national policies and determine all official strategies.
  • Command armies personally for major battles/campaigns.
  • Appoint, promote, or dismiss generals/admirals/spymasters.
  • Post all official government actions, military, budget, and diplomacy orders—with final say.
  • Coordinate with Admin (who acts as President/Congress/etc.) for all legislation, treaties, and law changes.

General or Admiral

  • Lead armies or fleets as assigned. Post movement & battle orders to proper threads.
  • Follow the leader's strategic goals or pursue your own at risk (may be relieved or replaced).
  • Reinforce, merge, split, and organize field units as needed. Ensure regiments/batteries match for reinforcements.
  • Promotions and dismissals are at the Leader's discretion.

Spymaster / Diplomat

  • Conduct official diplomatic actions in public threads or Admin-included PMs.
  • All agreements (even secret) must be posted with Admin included and summarized in national threads.
  • Monitor external communications and world events.
For full details or clarifications, see the Officer’s Manual or consult an Admin.
